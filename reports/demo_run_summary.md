The random forest was fitted only on folds with sufficient history, so its 360 rows cover a later period than the 420-row prior-only baseline. Their scores are not a same-period head-to-head comparison. The selected forecast falls back to that baseline when the ML history gate is not met.

The synthetic elevated-risk classifier scored on 420 historical rows with 30 positive labels. Its average precision was 0.477, Brier score 0.050, and recall 0.10 at the fixed 0.5 threshold. Probabilities remain explicitly uncalibrated and are hidden from analyst-facing prediction surfaces.

The nominal 90% yield interval covered 93.8% of 390 synthetic held-out rows, with mean width 0.87 tonnes/hectare. This is descriptive fixture behavior only; it is not evidence that real-world interval coverage is calibrated.

## Interpretation

The fixture deliberately encodes relationships between weather-like features and generated yield values. The model can therefore learn patterns that would not necessarily exist in real data. Synthetic scores must not be used in a resume as real-world forecast accuracy, a district risk ranking, or a case for operational release.
